Green Guard: the floor that only goes up

The goal is to end every month at or above ₹1,00,000. The floor rises as profit grows, and every buy is sized so a bad night cannot break it.
Paper trading only · no real money
An example month: value vs the floor (end of each day, illustrative)

The floor formula

Start: ₹99,500

Best profit reaches ₹500: ₹1,00,000

After that: ₹1,00,000 + 50% × (best profit − ₹500)

best ₹1,500 → ₹1,00,500
best ₹4,500 → ₹1,02,000

It never goes down, even into the next month.

Cushion: the only money at risk

Cushion = value − floor

  • Floor + ₹300: caution, no new buys
  • Floor + ₹100: tripwire, sell everything into LIQUIDBEES, no buys until tomorrow

Month-end rule: last 3 days only reduce, never add.

Sizing: every buy is budgeted for its worst night

Worst case = price gaps down past the stop overnight.
position risk = shares × price × (gap % + cost %)
