One decision every 60 seconds

The engine runs these 8 steps in order. No AI is involved. If a safety step fires, later steps do not run.
Paper trading only · no real money

1Read

Latest prices (must be under 3 minutes old), guidance.json, fast-lane orders, events, safe mode.

2Value and floor

Value = cash + LIQUIDBEES + positions, minus the cost to sell. If best profit grew, the floor ratchets up. It never goes down.

3Tripwire?

Value within ₹100 of the floor: sell everything into LIQUIDBEES. No buys until the next trading day.

4Exits

Stop-loss, sell half at +1.5% / +2.5% / +4.0%, trailing stop, 15:15 time and score exits, event and month-end exits.

5Triggers

Fast price move or urgent headline: block new buys for 30 minutes and wake the fast lane. No waiting for AI.

6Maybe buy

Only if every condition in the box below passes. Paper fill with costs and slippage.

7Log one line

BUY, SELL, SKIP, BLOCK or HOLD, with a plain-English reason.

8Save

portfolio.json and the tick row. Pushed to GitHub about every 5 minutes.

Step 6: a buy needs ALL of these (any one "no" = SKIP or BLOCK, with the reason logged)

  • Score above the threshold for 3 minutes in a row: gold 0.65 / silver 0.70 / copper 0.75 if "favour"; 0.70 / 0.75 / 0.80 if "neutral"; no buys on "block"
  • Price no more than 3 minutes old
  • Time 09:30 to 15:00 only; not in an event window (CPI, FOMC, RBI, results)
  • Not in the last 3 trading days of the month, safe mode, after a tripwire, or paused by a shock or fast-lane block
  • Cushion: value more than ₹300 above the floor
  • Risk budget: worst-case gap loss of all positions fits above the floor (3% gold, 5% silver, 7% copper)
  • Caps: gold 60%, silver 30%, copper 15% of value; at least 30% stays in LIQUIDBEES; at most 3 open positions
  • Limits: 3 buys a day, 1 per instrument a day, 3 per instrument a week, 30 minutes between buys
  • Calm: no re-buy after a stop-out the same day; no buys after 2 losing trades in a day

What a log line looks like

09:36 BUY GOLDBEES 40 @ ₹121.10
"The buy score for GOLDBEES stayed at 0.72 or higher (needs 0.65) for 3 minutes, and the risk budget allows 40 shares."

09:51 SKIP SILVERBEES
"The last buy was 15 minutes ago and at least 30 minutes must pass between buys."

Safety lines

Caution: floor + ₹300, no new buys. Tripwire: floor + ₹100, sell all.
