Memory: four layers, small on purpose

Python condenses everything. Claude reads a few KB, so each run costs the same in month 1 and month 12.
Paper trading only · no real money

Layer 1 · Raw logs

logs/decisions-D.jsonl, logs/ticks-D.csv

about 50 KB a day

Read by Python only. Never read by Claude.

Layer 2 · Live brief

memory/brief.md

at most 6 KB and 120 lines

Rewritten about every 5 minutes by Python. Only new headlines. Read by every routine.

Layer 3 · Daily note

memory/daily/D.md

at most 15 lines a day

Facts plus 1 to 3 "Lesson candidate:" lines. Written by the daily report. The last 3 are read.

Layer 4 · Lessons and weekly

memory/lessons.md, memory/weekly/WEEK.md

at most 40 bullets; weekly note at most 25 lines

Curated only by the Saturday review. Each lesson needs evidence.

Python summarises (free)
The daily report routine distils
The weekly review keeps only what is proven

Monthly archive (first Sunday, 02:00 IST)

  • Logs older than 35 days are gzipped into archive/YYYY-MM/
  • One summary row per day is kept in data/daily_summary.csv
  • Daily notes older than 60 days fold into archive/memory/

Other caps that keep it small

  • state/news.json: 48 hours and 400 items at most
  • Seen-headline memory: 2,000 entries or 7 days
  • Repo grows about 4 to 5 MB a month (about 50 to 60 MB a year), measured in the red-team review
